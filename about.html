About Us

Weather you can plan your life around.

Utsav Forecasts began with a simple idea: a calendar is where we plan our days, so the weather should live there too. The word utsav means “festival” or “celebration” in Nepali and Hindi, and that is the spirit of the project. Whether you are arranging a family gathering, a trek, a market day or a simple walk, knowing what the sky will do makes every occasion easier to enjoy. We bring together a clean monthly calendar and reliable forecast data so you can see both at a glance instead of switching between apps.

What you can do

Weather on every date

Each calendar day shows an icon with the expected high and low, covering the past week and the next fifteen days.

Hourly detail

Select a date to see hour-by-hour temperature and rain chances, plus wind, UV index, sunrise and sunset.

Any city, worldwide

Search for a city or use your current location. Your last location and preferences are remembered on your device.

Personal notes

Attach short notes or events to any day. They are stored privately in your browser and never leave your device.

How it works

  1. Your browser requests a forecast for the chosen location from the free Open-Meteo service.
  2. The data is arranged by date and drawn onto the monthly calendar grid.
  3. Clicking a date opens its full report, with an hourly strip and a place for your notes.

Built with

The site is written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks. Layout uses CSS Grid for the calendar and card sections and Flexbox for the navigation bar and page structure. Styles and scripts are kept in separate, reusable external files shared by every page.
